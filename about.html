WELCOME TO MOVIE REALITY

Movies are great. Wasting time is not.

A place made by a movie fan, for people who love movies and want to know whether a film is really worth their time.

Hi everyone.

Thank you for visiting Movie Reality.

I am a huge movie fan. I love watching movies, discovering new stories and getting lost in different worlds for a couple of hours.

But there is one thing I really do not like.

Spending two or three hours watching a movie and walking away thinking... "I just wasted my time."

So I created Movie Reality.

The idea is simple.

Before you spend your time watching a movie, Movie Reality helps you decide whether it is worth your time or a waste of it.

Our reviews are spoiler-light and focused on what actually matters to a viewer — the experience, the story, the entertainment and whether the movie delivers what it promises.

For people who love movies.

For people who discover movies every week.
For people who value their time.
For people who do not want to be disappointed after watching a film.
